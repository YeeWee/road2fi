Hey, welcome to Road to Fi.

I'll be honest — the idea of starting this blog has been circling in my head for a long time. Not because I have some impressive investing track record to show off, but because **I'm just an ordinary person trying to figure out how to build a more secure life for myself and the people I care about.**

There's one thing I truly believe: **financial freedom isn't a privilege reserved for the wealthy.** Ordinary people, through disciplined investing, good money habits, and a little patience, can absolutely get there. It's not an easy road — but it's a road worth taking.

## What Financial Independence Means to Me

For me, financial independence isn't about "I can buy whatever I want." It's much simpler than that —

**It means waking up every morning without the knot of anxiety about bills in my stomach.** It means working because I want to, not because I have to. It means one day being able to be present for my family — watching my kids grow up, spending time with my parents as they age — instead of being chained to a job I can't walk away from.

In plain terms: having enough reliable passive income to cover my living expenses, without having to sell my time and energy just to survive. That's the place I'm trying to reach.

## Why I'm Writing This Blog

A few reasons:

- **To keep myself honest.** Writing things down forces you to think clearly. I want to document what I've learned, the mistakes I've made, and the decisions that worked out — as a mirror for my own reflection.
- **To help others on the same path.** If you're also asking yourself, "How do I stop worrying about money all the time?" — I hope my experience gives you some direction or at least a sense that you're not alone.
- **To stay committed.** Putting something public is a kind of promise. It pushes me to keep learning and keep acting, instead of just talking about it.

I won't tell you "follow my picks and you'll get rich." What I will tell you is what I actually did, why I did it, and what happened — the wins and the losses alike.

## What You'll Find Here

- Value investing fundamentals and real-world thinking
- The power of compounding — and how to use it to design your life
- My personal investment decision process and post-trade reflections
- Honest thoughts about money, life, and what freedom actually feels like

This isn't a "get rich quick" place. It's a record of **patience, discipline, and long-term thinking.**

If you're on this road too, or just curious — welcome. Let's walk it together.
