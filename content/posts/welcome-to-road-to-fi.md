Welcome to Road to Fi. This is our journey toward Financial Independence.

We believe that ordinary people can achieve extraordinary financial freedom through disciplined investing and smart money habits.

## What is Financial Independence?

Financial Independence means having enough income — from investments, savings, or passive sources — to cover your living expenses for the rest of your life, without needing to work a traditional job.

## Why This Blog Exists

This blog exists to share our journey, lessons learned, and actionable insights for anyone walking the same path.
