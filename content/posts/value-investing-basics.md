Value investing is one of the most reliable paths to building long-term wealth. It's the approach Warren Buffett used to become one of the richest people in the world.

## What Is Value Investing?

Value investing means buying securities that appear undervalued by the market. You're looking for stocks trading below their intrinsic value — what the business is actually worth.

The key idea is simple: price is what you pay, value is what you get.

## Four Core Principles

Here are the fundamental principles every value investor should understand:

### Invest in What You Understand

Only buy businesses you can analyze and comprehend. If you can't explain how a company makes money in one sentence, don't own it.

### Margin of Safety

Always buy below intrinsic value. This buffer protects against:
- Mistakes in your analysis
- Bad luck or unexpected events
- Market volatility and irrational pricing

### Think Like a Business Owner

When you buy a stock, you're buying a piece of a real business. Treat it that way. Don't watch daily price fluctuations like a scoreboard.

### Let Compounding Work

Reinvest your earnings and hold quality businesses. The math is powerful:
- 10% annual returns double your money in 7.2 years
- 15% annual returns double your money in 4.8 years
- Time is the multiplier, patience is the strategy

## Getting Started

The best way to begin is to start reading. Study annual reports, understand business models, and practice calculating intrinsic value.

Remember: the stock market is a device for transferring money from the impatient to the patient.
